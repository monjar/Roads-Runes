2RPG direction — World · Quests · Journal · Character (Ride is a mode, not a tab)
What changed

Navigation from turn 1 (4a) stays as infrastructure. The home becomes the World: an ink-drawn map that only exists where you have ridden. Quests replace "suggested routes"; the Journal replaces History; the Character sits beside a quiet Cycling Profile that still governs safety and route suitability.

Ink map & fog

Explored cells render as a high-contrast sepia line map. Unexplored ground is cream with roads ghosting through at 15% so planning stays safe, and dashed ? markers hint at discoveries. Quest waypoints are diamonds, riders are circles, friends are initials — shape carries meaning before colour does.

Heraldic accents
Explorer · sage · compass star
Wizard · dusk violet · arcane ring
Warrior · terracotta · shield
Scribe · ink blue · drop

Two new hues derived in OKLCH at the accents' chroma. Class colour touches emblems, quest markers and XP bars only; the app stays cream.

0Cycling Companion — assumptions & system
Reading the brief

Flagship idea: describe the ride, get Relaxed / Adventure / Fast, understand why, ride from the wrist. Every screen below is judged on one question: does a beginner get it in a glance, and can an experienced rider dig deeper without being made to?

Sample content: south-east London loop from Rotherhithe through Greenwich Park and Chislehurst, pub stop at The Crown (26 km). Light mode first; dark mode and Watch use native dark.

Organic system, applied

Caprasimo only for screen titles and route names — the "companion voice". Figtree 700 for every number a rider reads while moving: numerals stay legible in sunlight and at 60px.

Cream ground, surface cards, pills. Terracotta is reserved for the route and the primary action; sage is the calm second voice (cycleways, "you are here", success). Maps are real OpenStreetMap tiles, washed like the system's photographs so the route pops.
